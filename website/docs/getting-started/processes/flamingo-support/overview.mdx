The Flamingo Team offers varying levels of support to product teams depending on the nature of
their requests.

The available support channels are the following:

### Request via Service Desk

Use our formal submission process via Service desk (link to be added) to create **asset requests**
(i.e. icons and illustrations), **component and variant requests** (i.e. new components or
improvements to existing ones) and **bug reports**.

[More on Request forms →](/getting-started/processes/flamingo-support/request-form)

### Slack Channel

Use the `#flamingo-design-feedback` channel for **quick questions** about Flamingo usage,
including **finding documentation** for specific components. This channel can also be used to
**preemptively discuss potential bugs** before submitting a formal bug report.

[More on the Slack channel →](/getting-started/processes/flamingo-support/slack-channel)

### Scheduled Office Hours

Book a calendar appointment (link to be added) for **moderate-complexity questions** about
Flamingo, best practice guidelines, UI, and Design System advice. These sessions may expand and
lead to collaborative pairing sessions if needed.

[More on the Open office hours →](/getting-started/processes/flamingo-support/office-hours)

### One-on-One/Pairing Sessions

Schedule a formal calendar invitation for **complex Flamingo inquiries**, brainstorming,
follow-up discussions on Design System topics, resolving inconsistencies, and **routine
collaboration between Flamingo designers and product designers** or their teams. This can also be
the first step to large cross-team initiatives that require multiple Flamingo designers: support
and timeline will then be evaluated on a case-by-case basis.

[More on the One-on-One pairing sessions →](/getting-started/processes/flamingo-support/pairing-session)
