Get direct feedback on your designs by a Flamingo team member, in order to ensure that they are
compliant with Flamingo styles and guidelines. The design review can also be used to clarify
doubts about Flamingo's components and brainstorm together.

## How to sign up

Join our open office hours on **Tuesdays** or **Thursdays**.

1. Sign up through our booking tool (link to be added).
2. Join the video call link (link to be added) at the time you scheduled slot.

## What to discuss during the open office hours

Flamingo Office Hours are a space for Designers to seek guidance on their work as it relates to
Design System topics.

- Align on which component to use
- Get support in creating a new component or modifying an existing one
- Review in-progress work for visual design, consistency, and QA
- Collaborate on component creation

## When is it best time to request a design review?

This depends on the type of review you are going to request:

1. If it's to get more knowledge on Flamingo and clarify doubts about Flamingo components and
   patterns, we recommend an early review in your design process. This is to ensure that you are
   all set up to create designs compliant to Flamingo's guidelines and make your work faster.
2. If you are already an advanced Flamingo user and you only need a quick review to validate your
   designs before handing them over to the engineers, we recommend scheduling a review before
   handing the designs over to the engineers for implementation.
3. During the implementation phase, you can ask Flamingo to support the engineers from your team
   and clarify any of their doubts. This can happen either offline or through the Flamingo open
   office hours as well. _This is extremely important in order to avoid UI bugs in production._
4. After the designs are implemented but before they are launched, you are advised to ask Flamingo
   for a design QA. The difference between a design review and QA is that the former reviews the
   designs, the latter reviews the implementations of those. For the design QA process, check the
   [QA page](/getting-started/processes/contribution/qa).
