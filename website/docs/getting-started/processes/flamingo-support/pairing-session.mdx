One-on-One pairing sessions are designed to help you effectively for complex Flamingo inquiries.
Whether you're addressing design system topics, resolving inconsistencies, or fostering cross-team
collaboration, this guide will provide a structured approach to ensure productive and organized
meetings.

## Getting Started

Before scheduling a meeting, clearly define the objectives. Is it for brainstorming, follow-up
discussions, or resolving design inconsistencies? Understanding the purpose will help determine
the necessary participants and the agenda.

Identify who needs to be involved. This usually includes Flamingo designers, but for larger
initiatives consider including other product designers, or entire teams.

Coordinate with all participants to find a date and time that accommodates everyone's schedule,
either via Slack or Google Calendar.

## How to Use It

Create a structured agenda that outlines the topics to be discussed and share the agenda with all
participants in advance, allowing them to prepare.

Gather and distribute any materials required for the meeting, such as design documents, reports,
or presentation slides. Providing these in advance allows participants to review and come prepared
with feedback or questions.

## When to Use It

Use this process when tackling **intricate design system topics** that require **a regular
comprehensive discussion** and input from the Flamingo designers, for which an Open office hour is
not enough to find a solution. A Pairing session can stem from an Open Office hour.

Pairing sessions can be valuable for large initiatives, like when inconsistencies in design or
implementation arise; organising a meeting to collaboratively find solutions and maintain design
system integrity represents the best approach to work fast and efficiently.

Moreover, this process can be used also as the first step in organizing large projects that
involve cross-team collaboration; support and timelines need evaluation on a case-by-case basis to
ensure proper resource allocation and project success.
