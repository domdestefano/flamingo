We encourage all squad designers and engineers to actively contribute to the Flamingo design
system.

To ensure its quality, please follow the following process:

| # | Process name | Description | By whom? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Request/proposal** | 1. Creation of a proposal of the new component or component change.<br />2. [Submission](/getting-started/processes/flamingo-support/request-form) to Flamingo team. | Squad designer |
| 2 | **Design creation** | 1. The Flamingo team refines it.<br />2. The Flamingo team adds it to the Figma library.<br />3. The Flamingo team creates guidelines for dev to meet all system standards. | Flamingo designer |
| 3 | **Development** | 1. Squad engineers pick up guidelines.<br />2. Squad engineers collaborate with the Flamingo engineers to develop component ensuring system standards.<br />3. [QA](/getting-started/processes/contribution/qa) component with Flamingo team (dev and design). | Squad engineer, Squad designer, Flamingo engineers, Flamingo designers |
| 4 | **Gallery/library update** | Add the updates to the component gallery Apps. | Squad engineer |
| 5 | **Documentation** | The Flamingo team will document the changes in ZeroHeight. | Flamingo designer |
