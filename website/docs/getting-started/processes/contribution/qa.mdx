Our quality assurance (QA) process guarantees that our library includes only high-quality and
accessible components. **Therefore, every team should request a QA session during the
implementation phase of a component or component change.**

## Process

1. Work together with the Flamingo engineers to ensure code consistency and best practices
   ([Team & comms](/getting-started/team-and-comms)).
2. Once your are "done" update the component library/App gallery and reach out to the Flamingo
   Designers for a visual QA ([Team & comms](/getting-started/team-and-comms)).
