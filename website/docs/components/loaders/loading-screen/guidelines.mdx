## Interactions

### Loading

The loading screen appears when a content is loading, or when after an action triggered the
whole UI to load a new state or new content. The loading interaction is made of:

- The scrim and the title, which both fade in/out.
- The loading spinner, which spins during the loading process.

The scrim appears as soon as the loading starts, and the loading spinner spins until the content
is loaded. When the content is loaded, both the scrim and the spinner fade out.

## Usage

**Loading screens** are used when the whole UI is loading new content or a complex action. The
loading UI prevents the user from interacting with the app until the loading is complete. Loading
screens need to be clear for the user, therefore the title has to clearly describe what the app
is doing.

That's why every loading screen must be made of:

- **Title**, to draw the user's attention and quickly describe what the app is loading.
- **Loading spinner**, to visually show that the app is running and loading.

Both elements are mandatory because the lack of them would frustrate the user, who will be
waiting for something to happen without having a clue about what the loading is doing or that is
even happening.

Loading screens fill the whole UI as the user is prevented from interacting with any element of
the app while the loading is happening; this includes navigation elements. **The loading screen
sits on top of the current screen; after the loading is complete, the loading screen fades out
and the new content appears right beneath it.**

## Do's and Dont's

### Detach

Absolutely avoid detaching our components or creating your own ones. Do not adopt components from
other design systems either. If you do, we'll find out and hunt you down and force you to use
Comic Sans for the rest of your design career.

Jokes aside, please use the components provided by our design system and don't detach them; if
you need different or improved ones, to cover your use cases, please contact the Flamingo design
system team and we will provide support and help.
