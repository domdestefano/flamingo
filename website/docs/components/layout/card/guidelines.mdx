## Do's and Dont's

### Card overkill

Cards should be used only when necessary, specifically to group relevant and actionable
information to scan at a glance. Try using white space to group and separate elements.

<DoDontTable
  doText="Use cards only when they make sense, to avoid visual heaviness."
  dontText="Avoid put everything in a card."
/>

<DoDontTable
  doText="Use cards to add prominence and separation from other elements."
  dontText="Do not overwhelm the UI with unnecessary elements."
/>

### Detach

Absolutely avoid detaching our components or creating your own ones. Do not adopt components from
other design systems either. If you do, we'll find out and hunt you down and force you to use
Comic Sans for the rest of your design career.

Jokes aside, please use the components provided by our design system and don't detach them; if
you need different or improved ones, to cover your use cases, please contact the Flamingo design
system team and we will provide support and help.

<DoDontTable
  doText="Use the cards as intended by the design system."
  dontText="If you need a variant to cover your use case, please contact us."
/>
