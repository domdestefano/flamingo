## Purpose & Strategic Usage

Illustrations should never be "just decoration." They need a job to do.

- **Humanize the Brand:** Use illustrations to inject personality and warmth,
  especially during moments of friction (like an error page).
- **Explain Complex Concepts:** Use visual metaphors to explain abstract
  ideas (e.g., "Session changes" or "Connection errors") that icons are too
  small to handle.
- **Celebrate Success:** Use more vibrant, energetic illustrations for "Empty
  States" or "Success Screens" to reward the user for completing a task.
- **The "Weight" Balance:** Illustrations should be visually heavier than
  icons but lighter than photography. Ensure they don't distract the user
  from the primary path.

## Representation & Inclusion

Illustrations are a powerful tool for making users feel seen and included.

- **Diversity by Design:** Reflect a range of ethnicities, body types, ages,
  and abilities.
- **Avoid Stereotypes:** Aim for authentic depictions of people. Don't rely
  on "generic" blue-skinned people unless it serves a specific stylistic
  purpose to remain neutral.
- **Cultural Sensitivity:** Ensure metaphors and gestures (like a "thumbs
  up") are appropriate for your global audience.
