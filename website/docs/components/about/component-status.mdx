## Guide for Zeroheight

On top of component pages, you can see the status of the component.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| 🔴 | To do / Not available |
| 🟠 | Outdated |
| 🟡 | WIP |
| 🟢 | Available |

For example:

| Figma | Web | iOS | Android |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 🟢 Available | 🟢 Available | 🔴 Not available | 🟢 Available |

:::info
Need a component that is 🔴 Not Available? Reach out to our flamingo team to discuss next steps.

Components can be built either by the Flamingo team or squads (with support/review).
:::
