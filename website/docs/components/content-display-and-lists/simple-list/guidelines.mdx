## Simple list with action vs. leading list

The difference between a simple list with an action element (button) and a leading list is what
happens next: a simple list's button triggers something to appear in place — like a modal or a
bottom sheet — while a leading list slides to another screen entirely (from the right, or from the
left if the user's settings are RTL).

## Do's and Dont's

### Action trigger vs. screen to screen

A simple list with an action element (button) triggers something to appear, like a modal or a
bottom sheet, meaning that it cannot lead to another screen. Use a leading list instead when you
want to navigate to another screen, sliding from the right side (or from the left if the user's
settings are RTL).

<DoDontTable
  doText="Use simple lists to trigger actions."
  dontText="If a list leads to another screen, use the leading list."
/>

### Detach

Absolutely avoid detaching our components or creating your own ones. Do not adopt components from
other design systems either. If you do, we'll find out and hunt you down and force you to use
Comic Sans for the rest of your design career.

Jokes aside, please use the components provided by our design system and don't detach them; if you
need different or improved ones, to cover your use cases, please contact the Flamingo design
system team and we will provide support and help.

  doText="Use the simple list as intended by the design system."
  dontText="If you need a variant to cover your use case, please contact us."
