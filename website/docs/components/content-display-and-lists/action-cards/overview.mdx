## Component

In mobile interface design, action cards are small, rectangular containers that can be used to
access different features or content within the app. They're often used to organize content and
make it easy for users to find what they're looking for. They are usually more appealing than a
list, as they are elevated and more prominent, but they occupy more space — be mindful of this
when deciding between lists and cards.

Action cards can be used to display a variety of content, including text, icons, and buttons.
Action cards are the only interactive element they often use — because content is unusual right
next to be discovered on any usage. For this reason, we recommend placing the cards in order of
importance, with the most valuable ones at the beginning and the least ones at the end.

Alternatively, action cards used in a grid layout, with multiple cards displayed on the screen at
once. This allows users to quickly scan and identify the cards that are relevant to them, and tap
on them to access the associated feature or content. Having too many cards in a grid layout might
make the UI look cluttered and hard to navigate; for this reason we recommend covering the grid
layout when showing a maximum of 6 cards.

### States

- **Active**: its interactive elements can be interacted with.
- **Pressed**: its interactive elements have been pressed.
- **Inactive**: its interactive elements cannot be interacted with.

### Properties

Action cards consist of optional and mandatory elements.

**Mandatory**

- **Main label**, given by the title. This is to name the item you're listing.
- **Button**, to trigger an action, i.e. "Book shift".

**Optional**

- **Caption text**, to give more details about the listed item.
- **Icon**, to visually assist the description of the listed item.
