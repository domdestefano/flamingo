# Guidelines

<ComponentTabs basePath="/components/content-display-and-lists/accordion" />

## Length of the content

Accordions are used when you want to hide/reveal content, so it's not necessary to show it with
prominence. Even though content is accessed on discovery, be mindful of the content's text length,
and clear about the user's experience with content. Remember that the user digests content in a
fast environment and looking at a phone screen.

<DoDontTable
  doText="Concise and clear content will give the user the important information they want to discover while revealing the content of the accordion."
  dontText="Too lengthy content might dissuade the user from reading all of it, and potentially prevent them from opening other accordions."
/>

## Detach

Absolutely avoid detaching our components or creating your own ones. Do not adopt components from
other design systems either — please use the components provided by our design system and don't
detach them. If you need a different or improved variant to cover your use case, please contact
the Flamingo design system team and we will provide support and help.

<DoDontTable
  doText="Use the accordion as intended by the design system."
  dontText="If you need a variant to cover your use case, please contact us instead of detaching or building your own."
/>
