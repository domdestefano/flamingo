# Guidelines

<ComponentTabs basePath="/components/overlay/action-picker" />

## Interactions

### Dismiss

The Action picker can be dismissed by tapping the Scrim (the area outside the bottom sheet).

### Appear / Disappear

The Action picker follows the behaviour of the Bottom sheet — it appears from the bottom of the
screen, and disappears to the bottom. The animation is linear.

## Usage

The Action picker sits always at the bottom of the screen. You cannot have an Action picker on
top of another one — they don't stack.

### Trigger point

Action pickers are typically triggered when you need to bury multiple actions in a menu, e.g.
when the user needs to upload a photo, which can happen via taking a photo, choosing a photo from
the gallery, or choosing a photo from a file. Flamingo recommends using a 3-dot icon to indicate
that the action will trigger an Action picker to appear.

## Use cases

### Destructive actions

Due to the nature of destructive actions, we recommend having only one per Action picker,
positioned last.

### Number of actions

Due to space limitations and the risk of cognitive overload, we recommend using a maximum of 4
actions. Avoid scrolling.
