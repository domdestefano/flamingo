# Content

<ComponentTabs basePath="/components/form/text-field" />

- Keep the Field title short and specific — describe what to enter, not an instruction ("Email," not "Enter your email").
- Write hint text as a short clarification, not a repeat of the title.
- Write error messages as what went wrong and, where possible, how to fix it.
