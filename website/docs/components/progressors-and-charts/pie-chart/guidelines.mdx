# Guidelines

<ComponentTabs basePath="/components/progressors-and-charts/pie-chart" />

## Placement

- The component can be placed on the background, on cards, overlays, or bottom sheets.
- Ensure there is enough space.

## Use cases

### Location

The Pie Chart is a highly visual component. Ensure it has enough space.

**Do:** Place it left aligned or when possible centered.

<CautionTable text="When placing it besides another element, ensure there is still enough white space." />

### Amount

The Pie Chart can have a minimum of 3 up to a maximum of 15 milestones, including the beginning and the end. This is to avoid visual clutter and frustration for the user.

<DoDontTable
  doImage="/img/components/pie-chart/pie-chart-amount-do.png"
  doText="An appropriate number of milestones can engage the user in the challenge or quest to achieve completion and receive a reward."
  dontImage="/img/components/pie-chart/pie-chart-amount-dont.png"
  dontText="Too many milestones can give the user a sense of frustration from the excessive length of the challenge or quest, and can ultimately lead to giving up."
/>

### Content

Ensure the type of the chart matches the message (see the Overview tab) and the content explains what the chart is displaying.

<DoDontTable
  doText="The upper label explains what the bar is about and the lower one shows the actual value."
  dontText="Avoid ambiguous language, and don't display the chart without context."
/>
