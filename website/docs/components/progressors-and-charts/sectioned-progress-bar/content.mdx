## Amount of milestones

The sectioned progress bar should have a maximum of 10 milestones, including the ending one. This
is to avoid visual clutter in the progress bar, but also to avoid frustration in the user. If you
need to show more than 10 milestones, please evaluate other options or reach out to the Flamingo
team for ad hoc cases.

Every milestone should be equally distanced from the previous and the upcoming one.

## Iconography

- You can choose freely the icons for the milestones. Be mindful of the small size of the icon and
  the clarity of the message you want to convey.
- Do not overwhelm the user with too many additional visuals.
- Never mix styles (i.e. icons & illustrations).

## Labels

Every milestone can have a label to indicate what the milestone is about.

If a sectioned progress bar has more than 5 milestones, we suggest to only display:

- **the starting one**, to show where the user's challenge or quest has begun
- **the current one**, to reference the current status of the user's challenge or quest
- **the ending one**, to give a glimpse of what the user will achieve on completion

## Truncation behaviour

The labels don't truncate.
